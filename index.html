PROTOCOL 01 // NUTRITION

EAT LIKE A
PREDATOR

Two meals. Carnivore-leaning. Engineered for satiety, hormones & recomposition.
RULE 01

FAST UNTIL 12:00

No food before noon. Allowed: water, sparkling water, sugar-free sodas, black coffee, plain tea, electrolytes (no calories).

This is the easy part. Don't make it complicated.

RULE 02

TWO MEALS / DAY

Meal 1: ~12:00. Meal 2: ~19:00–20:00. That's it. No snacks. No grazing. No "just a little something."

If you're hungry between meals — drink water and shut up.

RULE 03

MEAT FIRST. ALWAYS.

Every meal starts with protein. Hit at least 250–400g cooked meat per meal. Eat protein until full before anything else touches the plate.

EAT THIS

THE GREEN LIGHT

Whole, unprocessed, dense. Eat freely.

  • Ribeye, sirloin, ground beef (80/20)
  • Chicken thighs > chicken breast
  • Eggs (whole, with yolk)
  • Avocado (1–2 per day)
  • Almonds, macadamias, walnuts
  • Salt — heavy. Real salt, not table junk.
  • Butter, ghee, tallow for cooking
  • Water — minimum 3L / day
DO NOT EAT

THE RED LIGHT

If you eat this, you've broken the protocol. Restart the day.

  • Bread, rice, pasta, oats, all grains
  • Sugar in any form (incl. honey, agave)
  • Seed oils (canola, sunflower, soybean)
  • Pork, fish, lamb (this round — keep it tight)
  • Alcohol — zero, all 28 days
  • Fruit juice, smoothies, oat milk
  • Anything labeled "low-fat" or "diet"
  • Snacks between meals

PROTOCOL 02 // TRAINING

TRAIN TO
FAILURE

4 full-body days. Compound lifts. Every working set to true muscular failure.
// TRAINING RULES OF ENGAGEMENT
Warm-up
2 ramping sets per first compound. Work up to working weight. Warm-ups are NOT to failure.
Working Sets
Last set of every exercise = absolute failure. Can't move the bar. No exceptions.
Rest Periods
2–3 min between compound sets. 60–90s on isolation. Time it. Don't scroll.
Progression
Beat last week's log. Add 2.5kg OR 1 rep on the failure set. Track everything in a notebook.
Form
Full ROM. Controlled eccentric (2–3s down). No bounce. No ego. Failure with bad form doesn't count.
Cardio
10,000 steps daily. That's it. No HIIT, no running. Walk. Recovery is sacred.

PROTOCOL 04 // DOCTRINE

RULES OF
EXECUTION

Break any of these and you've broken the protocol. Restart the day.
01

SLEEP IS THE LIFT

7–9 hours, every night. Phone out of the bedroom. Cold, dark room. If you sleep 5 hours, you didn't train — you damaged tissue you can't repair.

02

FAILURE OR FAKE

If the last rep moves smooth, you didn't fail. Failure means the bar stops mid-rep with full effort. No cheating, no "I had one more in me."

03

TRACK EVERY REP

Notebook or notes app. Date, exercise, weight, reps. If you're not tracking, you're guessing. Guessing doesn't grow muscle.

04

WEIGH IN DAILY

Same time. Same conditions. Right after the bathroom, before water. Track the 7-day average — that's the real number. Daily fluctuations are noise.

05

PHOTO WEEKLY

Front, side, back. Same lighting, same time of day. The mirror lies. The scale lies. Photos don't.

06

NO ALCOHOL. ZERO.

One beer destroys 36 hours of recovery and tanks testosterone. 28 days. You can survive. If you can't, the program isn't your problem.

07

WALK 10K STEPS

Daily. Non-negotiable. Best fat-loss tool ever invented. Doesn't tax recovery. Costs nothing. No excuses.

08

HUNGRY ≠ EMERGENCY

Hunger is data. Not a command. Drink water, salt your tongue, get back to work. The first 5 days are the hardest. After that, the body adapts.

09

SHOW UP UGLY

Tired? Train. Stiff? Train. Bad day at work? Train harder. The session you don't want to do is the one that builds you. Discipline > motivation. Always.
